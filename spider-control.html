Common in Northeast Alabama

Spiders we treat in Scottsboro.

Most spiders we see in this area are a nuisance — but some deserve extra attention.

Common house spiders, wolf spiders, jumping spiders, and cellar spiders are frequent visitors to Scottsboro homes. They are mostly a nuisance, but their presence usually means other insects are around for them to eat.

Brown recluse spiders are the one species in our area that can bite people. Because they hide in undisturbed places, they need a specialized approach. We offer brown recluse service as a separate add-on to the general pest plan.

If you are seeing a lot of spiders, the real question is: what are they eating? Find that, and you solve the spider problem.

Why they keep coming back

Why spiders show up.
And how to stop it.

Spiders follow food, shelter, and moisture. Change those conditions and you change the spider problem.

They're hunting. Spiders set up where insects are plentiful. If you're seeing spiders, other pests are usually nearby — which is why we treat the whole picture, not just the webs.

They're looking for shelter. Gaps around doors and windows, clutter against the foundation, firewood, and undisturbed corners all give spiders a place to hide. We point out conditions you can change and sealing needs we can quote separately.

Moisture attracts them. Damp areas — crawl spaces, basements, bathrooms, and laundry rooms — draw both spiders and the insects they eat. Our inspection includes a look at these areas.

Your year-round plan keeps this pressure on through all four seasons, with monthly exterior service during May, June, July, and August at no extra charge.

Straight answers

Spider questions, answered.

Good service starts with a clear conversation — and continues with one.

Are spiders covered in the general pest plan?

Yes. Common nuisance spiders are included in our year-round general pest protection. Brown recluse service is a specialized add-on because it requires a different approach.

Will I still see spiders after treatment?

Possibly. Treatment removes what's there and reduces activity, but spiders can wander in from outside, especially in warmer months. Tell us what you're seeing and we'll respond — follow-up visits for covered pests are included.

Are the treatments safe for kids and pets?

We place products strategically in areas where pests travel and hide, rather than treating indiscriminately. Tell us about children, pets, aquariums, or sensitivities so we can discuss the appropriate precautions for your home.

How often should spider treatment be done?

Quarterly service keeps webs from building back and treats the insects spiders eat. During May–August we visit monthly for exterior service — included in the plan.

Do you treat brown recluse spiders?

Yes — as a specialized add-on service. Because brown recluse spiders hide in undisturbed areas and can bite, they need a targeted approach beyond the general pest plan. Ask us for details.
