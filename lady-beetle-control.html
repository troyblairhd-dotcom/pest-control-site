Lady Beetle

Beneficial in a garden, a nuisance in a house. Multicolored Asian lady beetles cluster indoors to overwinter and leave yellow stains when handled roughly.

Threat level: Low Active: Fall–Spring Jackson County

Signs you have lady beetles

  • Clusters of beetles in windows and attic spaces
  • Yellow staining on walls and curtains
  • Activity on warm winter days

How to prevent them

  • Seal gaps around windows, soffits, and utility entries
  • Vacuum rather than crush to avoid staining
  • Apply a barrier treatment in early fall

When to call a professional

Lady Beetle is often manageable with prevention alone, but recurring activity indoors means the source hasn't been addressed. If you're seeing them repeatedly after cleaning and sealing entry points, an inspection will identify where they're coming from and stop the cycle.
