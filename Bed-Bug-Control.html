Why We Don't Recommend Heat Treatment

Heat treatment sounds good — but it doesn't last

  • Bed bugs can survive heat treatment by hiding on the other side of insulation, where the heat simply doesn't penetrate
  • Surviving bugs return to re-infest your home, often within the same year
  • Bed bugs can live without a blood meal for up to one full year, meaning even a mostly successful heat treatment can fail when hidden bugs emerge months later
  • Heat treatment is expensive, disruptive, and offers no residual protection — once the heat is gone, there's nothing stopping bed bugs from coming back

We take a different approach — one designed for long-term control, not just a quick fix that lets the problem return.

The Aprehend Difference

Aprehend — Designed for Bed Bug Elimination

Aprehend is a professional biopesticide specifically designed to eliminate bed bug infestations and prevent their reoccurrence. Unlike heat treatment or traditional sprays that only kill on contact, Aprehend uses a natural fungal spore that bed bugs pick up and carry back to their hiding places — spreading the treatment deep into walls, behind insulation, and into cracks where sprays and heat can't reach.

Once applied, Aprehend continues working for months, creating a long-lasting barrier that eliminates bed bugs long after we've left your home.
