Termites we protect against.

Two subterranean species threaten homes in our area — and one of them is new to us.

Eastern Subterranean Termites are the most common termite in the United States and the primary threat to Scottsboro homes. They live in large underground colonies and build mud tubes to reach wood — often entering through foundations, slab edges, and crawl spaces. They work silently for years before damage becomes visible.

Formosan Termites have newly surfaced in our area, and they demand attention. Formosan colonies are far larger and more aggressive than Eastern Subterranean colonies, and they can chew through wood faster and in greater numbers. Our treatment plan is built to handle both species.

Termites don't announce themselves. They eat quietly, for years, until one day the damage is visible. The only way to know is to look.

The work behind the results

How termite protection works.
Step by step.

Free inspection first. Then a two-part defense that protects your home and eradicates the colony.

01 / FREE INSPECTION

See what's really there.

We inspect your home — foundation, slab edges, crawl space, wood-to-soil contact, and moisture conditions. We look for mud tubes, damaged wood, and swarm evidence. You get a clear report and an exact cost, with no obligation.

02 / TARGETED LIQUID TREATMENT

Termidor HE barrier.

We apply Termidor HE as a targeted liquid treatment around your foundation — trenching and rodding where needed to create a continuous, non-repellent barrier. Termites can't detect it, so they cross it freely, pick up the product, and carry it back to the colony. The transfer effect eliminates termites where they live.

03 / TRELONA BAIT STATIONS

Intercept & eradicate.

We install Trelona bait stations around your perimeter. Foraging termites find the slow-acting bait and share it throughout the colony. Because the bait works gradually, termites don't associate it with danger — and the colony is eradicated over time.

04 / MONITOR & MAINTAIN

Keep the defense active.

We monitor the bait stations and the barrier on schedule, checking for activity and refreshing the system as needed. Termite protection isn't a one-time event — it's an ongoing defense we maintain for you.

Why both? The Termidor HE liquid barrier protects your home immediately and continuously. The Trelona bait stations hunt the colony itself — eradicating termites at the source. Liquid alone protects; bait alone eradicates. Together, they give you protection and eradication. Your free inspection determines the exact treatment plan and cost for your home.

Why they're a serious threat

Why termites deserve
your attention now.

Termites are the most destructive pest a homeowner can face — and the easiest to miss.

They work silently. Subterranean termites live underground and enter your home through foundations and slab edges. They build mud tubes and eat wood from the inside out. By the time damage is visible, it's often been years in the making — and thousands of dollars in repairs.

Formosan termites change the picture. The Formosan termite, newly surfaced in our area, is more aggressive and builds far larger colonies than native species. It can consume wood faster and in greater volume — which is why our treatment plan is designed for both species.

Alabama is high-risk territory. Our warm, humid climate is ideal for subterranean termites. Nearly every home in our area is at risk, whether or not termites have been seen. A free inspection tells you where you stand.

Termite damage is rarely covered by homeowners insurance. The cheapest protection is a professional inspection — and it's free. Call us and know where your home stands.

Straight answers

Termite questions, answered.

Good service starts with a clear conversation — and continues with one.

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. We offer a free termite inspection to anyone who may need this service. We assess your home, identify any activity or risk factors, and calculate an exact cost. No obligation.

What is Termidor HE?

Termidor HE is a professional-grade, non-repellent liquid termiticide. Applied as a targeted treatment around your foundation, it creates a continuous barrier termites can't detect. Termites that contact it carry it back to the colony — the transfer effect — which eliminates termites at the source.

What are Trelona bait stations?

Trelona bait stations are installed around your home's perimeter. Foraging termites find the slow-acting bait and share it throughout the colony, eradicating the colony over time. They're a key part of colony-level control.

Do I need both the liquid treatment and bait stations?

The liquid barrier protects your home immediately; the bait stations eradicate the colony itself. Together they provide protection and eradication — the strongest defense. We'll recommend the right plan for your home after your free inspection.

Is the treatment safe for my family and pets?

Termidor HE is applied as a targeted treatment in the soil around your foundation — not broadcast through your living space. Trelona stations are placed in the ground around your perimeter. We follow all label requirements and discuss any precautions based on your home and household.

What are the signs of termites?

Mud tubes on foundations or walls, hollow-sounding wood, discarded wings near windows and doors, and swarmers in spring. If you see any of these — or just want peace of mind — schedule a free inspection.
